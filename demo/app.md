# The app: a shared shopping list

A household shares one shopping list. Anyone in the household can add items, tick them off when they're bought, and see who added what.

## Building blocks

- **List:** the one shared list of a household.
- **Items:** each has a name, an amount, who added it, and whether it's bought.
- **Members:** the people in the household. Each one signs in with their own account.
