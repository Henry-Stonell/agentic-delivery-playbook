# Decision library

Decisions already made for the shopping list. The plan reviewer checks every plan against them. A plan that goes against one needs a new decision first.

1. **Items are never deleted.** They're ticked off, and ticked items are hidden after 30 days. People want to see what was bought.
2. **Amounts are a whole number and a unit from a fixed list:** pieces, grams, kilograms or litres. Free text made amounts impossible to add up.
3. **Only members of a household can see or change its list.** Lists are private.
4. **Every change records who made it and when.** "Who added this?" is the most common question.
5. **No prices.** The app is about what to buy, not what it costs.
6. **It works the same on phone and computer.** No features for only one of them.
