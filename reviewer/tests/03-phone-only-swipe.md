Mistake: goes against decision 6 (same on phone and computer)
---
Quicker ticking on the phone

In the shop I want to tick things off faster with one hand.
---
## Plan

### What was asked
A faster way to tick items off on the phone.

### What we will build
On phones, swiping an item to the right ticks it off. Computers keep the checkbox only, because there is no swipe there. This belongs to the Items building block.

### What it touches
- The list screen on phones

### Decisions this relies on
- 4: ticking off still records who did it and when.

### What we will not do
No swipe to untick.

### Open questions
None.

### How we will test it
A visual test on a phone screen size that swipes an item.
