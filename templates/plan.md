# Plan (template, posted as a comment on the issue)

The developer's agent fills this in after the joint analysis and posts it on the issue. The idea agent reviews it.

## What was asked
One or two sentences, in the requester's terms. Link the issue.

## What we will build
The approach, in plain words. Which building block does this belong to?

## What it touches
- Tables / data models:
- Screens / endpoints:
- Work areas (and their owners). Is any of them frozen?

## Decisions this relies on
Links to entries in the team's decision library, or "none found".

## What we will not do
Out of scope, so the reviewer doesn't have to guess.

## Open questions
For the requester or an area owner. Tag the person.

## How we will test it
Visual user test, unit tests, security checks, integration tests.
